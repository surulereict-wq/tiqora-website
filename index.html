Payment integration

Every ticket and every vote starts with a confirmed payment.

We connect payment collection to your ticketing and voting pages, so each payment is matched to what was bought.

STEP 1 Buyer or voter pays On the ticket page or voting page, by card, transfer or mobile payment.
STEP 2 Payment is confirmed The payment provider confirms the transaction before anything is issued.
STEP 3 Ticket issued or vote counted The buyer gets a digital ticket, or the vote is added to the live count.
STEP 4 Funds settled to the organiser Clear records show what was sold and what is due to you.
